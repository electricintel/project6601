Value Engine Prototype

Early conceptual model for analyzing Form 6601 declarations.

Purpose

The Value Engine Prototype evaluates Form 6601 declarations in terms of clarity, burden-neutrality, transparency, and fairness.

Input Dimensions
Clarity
How clearly the value is described.
Burden-Neutrality
Absence of harmful classification.
Transparency
Visibility of context and intent.
Fairness
Respect for all parties involved.
Prototype Scoring
Clarity:           [ 0 – 10 ]
Burden-Neutrality: [ 0 – 10 ]
Transparency:      [ 0 – 10 ]
Fairness:          [ 0 – 10 ]
