What is Project6601?

Project6601 defines Form 6601 — a clean, transparent, burden-neutral declaration of value for individuals, cooperatives, and communities. It is designed to avoid exploitative classification and to align with fairness and freedom.

Why It Matters

Existing systems often force people into categories that create risk, stigma, or unfair treatment. Project6601 offers a different path: a declaration of value that respects human dignity and economic reality without weaponizing classification.
