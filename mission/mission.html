Our Mission

Building a clean, fair, freedom-aligned declaration of value for everyone.

Why Project6601 Exists

Around the world, individuals and cooperatives struggle to declare their value without being forced into burdensome categories, exploitative classifications, or systems designed to benefit intermediaries instead of people.

Project6601 exists to fix that. We create a transparent, burden-neutral, classification-clean declaration of value that respects human dignity, economic fairness, and personal freedom.

Who This Helps

  • Individuals seeking a clean way to declare value
  • Cooperatives and small groups needing fairness
  • Creators, builders, and contributors
  • Anyone harmed by classification-based systems

What We Stand Against

  • Exploitative classification systems
  • Burdened declarations that create risk or stigma
  • Opaque value reporting frameworks
  • Intermediaries who profit from human labor unfairly

Our Value Promise

Project6601 promises a declaration of value that is: clean, fair, transparent, burden-neutral, and freedom-aligned.
